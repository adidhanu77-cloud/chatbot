# East Point College AI Chatbot

An AI-powered chatbot developed to simplify and streamline the admission and query process for East Point College. The chatbot provides instant responses regarding academic programs, eligibility requirements, fee structures, campus facilities, and contact details.

---

## 📌 Features

* **Admission Guidance:** Quick responses to queries about admission procedures and eligibility criteria.
* **Course & Department Info:** Detailed insights into offered undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs.
* **Fee Structure & Scholarships:** Instant updates on course fees and available scholarship opportunities.
* **Campus Facilities & Placements:** Information regarding hostels, campus infrastructure, and placement records.
* **Contact & Support:** Instant access to department contact details and administration helplines.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
* **Deployment:** GitHub Pages
